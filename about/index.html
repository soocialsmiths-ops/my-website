About Social Smiths · Mumbai

Built by creators, driven by results

Social Smiths is a full-service creative marketing agency built exclusively for real estate. From strategy to cinematic production to influencer distribution — we handle everything so your brand becomes the benchmark.

Our story

A cinematographer and a strategist, on one team

Operating from Ghatkopar West, Mumbai, and shooting across India and Dubai, we bring global sensibility to India's most ambitious developments. We don't just make content — we build brands that move property.

Social Smiths was founded by Brendan Dsouza, a Mumbai real estate cinematographer, alongside Prarit Singh, a brand and digital strategist. That's the difference: your content is made by people who spend their week on site at real projects, not a generalist team learning real estate on your budget.
