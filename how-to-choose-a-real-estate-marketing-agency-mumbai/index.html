How to Choose a Real Estate Marketing Agency in Mumbai

Mumbai has hundreds of agencies that say they do real estate. Very few are built for it. This guide covers the questions worth asking, the red flags worth walking away from, and how to match the agency to what your launch actually needs.

By Brendan Dsouza, co-founder of Social Smiths · Real estate cinematographer, 4 years on Mumbai sites

Step one

Start with what you actually need

"Marketing" means four different jobs in real estate. Most agencies are strong at one or two. Decide which matters most for this project before you shortlist anyone.

Launch buzz

A new project needs attention fast — films, reels and creator content that make people talk about it in the first 30 days.

Steady enquiries

An ongoing project needs a predictable flow of qualified leads, usually driven by paid ads and a tight follow-up process.

Brand perception

Premium and luxury developers need content that justifies the price per square foot — craft matters more than volume here.

Channel partner support

Brokers and CPs need a regular supply of shareable creatives they can push to their own buyer networks.

Step two

7 questions to ask before you sign

01

Do you work only in real estate?

A specialist already understands buyer psychology, micro-markets and possession timelines. A generalist learns them on your project.

02

Who actually shoots the content?

In-house shooters know your brand and move fast. Outsourced production adds a layer between you and the person holding the camera.

03

Can you show projects like mine?

Ask for work in your segment and micro-market. A Worli luxury tower and a Panvel township need very different content.

04

How do you measure success?

The answer should be site visits and qualified enquiries. If it's only views and followers, keep looking.

05

Who runs my account day to day?

Senior people often pitch and then disappear. Meet the person you'll actually work with.

06

Where do your influencers come from?

Ask whether they have real relationships with property creators, or buy from generic lists. The difference shows up in who the audience actually is.

07

How do you handle MahaRERA compliance?

Promotional material for registered projects must carry the correct registration details. An experienced agency builds this in without being reminded.

Walk away if you see these

Red flags

Guaranteed numbers

No honest agency can promise a fixed number of leads or bookings before seeing your project, pricing and location.

Stock footage

If the portfolio is mostly stock clips and renders, ask who will film your actual site.

Copy-paste calendars

The same content plan for every client means nobody has thought about your buyer.

Vanity-only reports

Reports full of impressions and likes, with no enquiry or site-visit data, hide whether anything is working.

Long lock-ins, no trial

A confident agency will agree to a defined first phase before a long commitment.

No micro-market knowledge

If they can't tell Thane West from Ghodbunder Road buyers, they can't write for either.

Know the landscape

The four types of agency you'll meet

Performance agencies

Best for steady lead volume through Google and Meta ads. Usually lighter on original content and on-ground shoots.

Creative & content studios

Best for films, reels and brand perception. Check whether they can also run distribution, or only produce.

Influencer agencies

Best for launch reach and credibility. Many work across fashion, food and finance, so ask how much property work they have done.

Full-service agencies

One partner across content, social, influencers and ads. Convenient, but confirm which parts are done in-house.

Budgeting

What drives the cost

Most Mumbai agencies price real estate work as a monthly retainer or a per-launch fee. Four things move that number the most:

Shoot days

On-site filming, drone and crew time is usually the largest production cost.

Monthly deliverables

The number of reels, films, carousels and posts committed each month.

Influencer tier

Nano, micro and macro creators price very differently; the mix shapes the budget.

Media management

Running paid ads adds a management fee. Ad spend itself is billed separately.

Questions

Frequently asked

What does a real estate marketing agency do?

It plans and produces the marketing that sells a project: launch strategy, property films and reels, social media, influencer campaigns, paid ads and lead handling. Some agencies cover all of it; many specialise in one or two of these.

How much does a real estate marketing agency in Mumbai cost?

Most work on a monthly retainer or a per-launch project fee. The price is driven by shoot days, the number of deliverables each month, the tier of any influencers involved, and whether media spend is managed. Ad spend is almost always billed separately from the agency fee.

Should I hire a specialist or a general digital agency?

If real estate is your core business, a specialist usually ramps up faster. They already know buyer psychology, micro-markets and MahaRERA requirements, so you spend less time briefing and correcting.

How long before I see results?

Content and influencer work can drive enquiries within the first few weeks of a launch. Organic brand building and search visibility typically take three to six months to compound.

What should an agency report on?

Site-visit bookings, qualified enquiries and cost per enquiry — not just views, likes and followers. Ask to see a sample report before you sign.
