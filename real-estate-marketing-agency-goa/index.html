The Goa market

In Goa, you're selling a lifestyle

Goa property is bought mostly by people who don't live there yet — families and investors from India's big cities looking for a holiday home or a villa that earns rental income. Hotels and villa stays sell to the same audience. They decide from what they see on Instagram and YouTube, so the film has to make them feel the place. We shoot villas, holiday homes and hotels in Goa for exactly that audience.
